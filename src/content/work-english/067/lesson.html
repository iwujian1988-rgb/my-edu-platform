Effective meetings have a clear goal, a short list of things to cover, and the right mix of attendees. If you decide to have a meeting, below. When you’re done, double-check: Does everyone on the agenda really need to be there, or could they get the notes via email later?

In your invite, briefly summarize your points from the meeting prep worksheet above. If you’re working with people who like to prepare in advance, or if you’re trying to gather feedback asynchronously before the meeting, be sure to . Is your invite too wordy? Try cutting it by half. Bonus points for including one of the 3d’s in your meeting title (ex: product design decision, team goals discussion, product roadmap debate).

Once the meeting is on the calendar, help . Your meeting will run more smoothly if the team is prepped and ready for the discussion. Read and share any relevant content beforehand so everyone has the same context. Make sure everyone has done their pre-meeting work. Send a friendly reminder if necessary. If you’d like anyone to share their screen, let them know ahead of time so they have the right documents handy. Think about the tone you’d like to set. For example, a team-building meeting should feel light and fun, whereas a check on well-being should feel safe and supportive.

If you called the meeting, take advice from The Art of Gathering author Priya Parker and act as a gracious, authoritative host—someone who’s welcoming and warm yet confidently in charge. When your meeting starts: Leave time for unscripted chitchat. If your team members are remote, chances are they miss making impromptu connections. Warmly welcome everyone by their first name. If people don’t know each other, ask them to introduce themselves. Awkward silence? Try a creative conversation starter. Briefly restate your goal and agenda, and what happened the last time you met.If needed, give people a short “study hall” at the beginning to review reference docs. Encourage honesty and candor by creating a safe and confidential place for people to feel comfortable sharing their thoughts and ideas.

When we’re videoconferencing, it’s easy to space out or interrupt while someone else is talking. No matter your role, you can help ensure that all voices—not just the loudest—are heard in your meeting. Try to: Explicitly say you’d like to hear everyone’s perspective. Redirect when things go off topic: “JD, I love those ideas but I want to make sure we get through our agenda. Let’s put them in the parking lot.”Interrupt the interrupters: “Thanks, Justin, but I’d like to finish hearing from Eunice. Eunice, can you say more?”Call on quieter people: “I’d love to hear from folks who haven’t spoken yet. Monique, what do you think?”

When the meeting’s over, thank everyone for their time. Then quickly summarize the decision you made, what happens next, and who will do what. Have a notetaker prep a short recap and send it after. Bonus points for ending five minutes early, so people have more time to recharge. More resources

01 在你本能地安排虚拟会议之前，请确保它是为了3D——重要决策、辩论和讨论。如果你是在做状态更新或只是有一个快速问题，尝试异步处理。查看《会议101》了解更多信息。

02 有效的会议有明确的目标、简短的待办事项清单以及合适的与会者组合。如果你决定召开会议，请填写下面的会议准备表。完成后，再次检查：议程上的每个人都真的需要参加吗，或者他们之后可以通过邮件获取会议记录？

03 在你的邀请中，简要总结你来自上述会议准备表的要点。如果你与喜欢提前准备的人合作，或者你想在会议前异步收集反馈，请务必包含带有清晰说明的参考文档。你的邀请是否过于冗长？尝试将其精简一半。在会议标题中包含3D之一可获得额外加分（例如：产品设计决策、团队目标讨论、产品路线图辩论）。

04 一旦会议被安排在日程上，帮助让每个人——包括你自己——做好准备。如果团队成员已准备好并进行讨论，你的会议将进行得更顺利。提前阅读和分享任何相关内容，以便大家有相同的背景信息。确保每个人都完成了会前工作。如有必要，发送友好的提醒。如果你希望任何人共享屏幕，请提前告知他们，以便他们手头有正确的文档。思考你想要设定的基调。例如，团队建设会议应该感觉轻松有趣，而健康检查会议应该感觉安全和支持。

情景练习（模拟） · Making Remote Meetings Productive

以下人物与情节为模拟内容，用于练习表达。

Sarah, a project manager, needs to coordinate with her team across three time zones to finalize the quarterly marketing campaign.

Her team members are frustrated with back-to-back virtual meetings that run over time and lack clear agendas, causing delays in their actual work.

Sarah implements a structured approach by sending pre-meeting materials with specific discussion points and time limits, resulting in a focused 30-minute session that achieves all objectives.

查看场景中文

Sarah，一位项目经理，需要与跨越三个时区的团队合作，敲定季度营销活动方案。

她的团队成员对连续不断的远程会议感到沮丧，这些会议经常超时且缺乏明确议程，导致实际工作延误。

Sarah通过发送包含具体讨论点和时间限制的会前材料，实施了一种结构化方法，最终在30分钟的专注会议中达成了所有目标。

reflexively

原文词汇
/rɪˈfleksɪvli/　adverb
查看释义、搭配与用法

反射地；本能地；不假思索地

reflexively schedule/do something

Don't reflexively agree to every request without considering your priorities.

不要不假思索地同意每项请求而不考虑你的优先事项。

表示不经过思考的、自动的反应，常用于提醒人们避免习惯性的、未经思考的行为。

原文词汇
/eɪˈsɪŋkrənəsli/　adverb

Our team handles most communications asynchronously to respect everyone's focus time.

我们团队以异步方式处理大多数沟通，以尊重每个人的专注时间。

指不要求同时参与的工作方式，与同步(synchronously)相对，在远程工作中常见。

05

语法拆句

原文句子

Before you reflexively schedule a virtual meeting, make sure it’s for the 3d’s—important decisions, debates, and discussion.

  • Before you reflexively schedule a virtual meeting 是时间状语从句，其中 reflexively 是副词修饰 schedule，表示'不假思索地'，整个从句表示'在你不假思索地安排虚拟会议之前'
  • make sure it's for the 3d's 是主句，其中 make sure 是'确保'的意思，it's for 表示'是为了...的目的'，破折号后的内容是对 3d's 的解释说明
  • important decisions, debates, and discussion 是三个并列名词短语，由逗号和 and 连接，共同构成 3d's 的具体内容，表示'重要决定、辩论和讨论'
换到工作场景

Send the agenda before the call, keep the meeting to 30 minutes, and end by confirming each action owner and due date.

06

句式仿写

比较两种写法，再试着换成自己的工作内容。

原来的写法

Let’s invite the whole department and work out the meeting goal once everyone is there.

修改后的写法

Could we meet to decide whether to replace the support tool? I’ll share the key questions in the invite and include only the people who need to advise on or make the decision. Let’s end with an owner and next step.

我们能否开会决定是否更换客服工具？我会在邀请中写明目标和关键问题，并邀请需要提供意见或作出决定的人。会议结束时，我们确定负责人和下一步。

试着替换信息
Could we meet to decide [the question]? I’ll include [the goal and key questions] and invite [the necessary people]. Let’s end with [an owner and next step].
