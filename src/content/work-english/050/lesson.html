04

语法拆句

原文句子

We haven’t concluded yet exactly how many other roles will be impacted (we know that there will be reductions in our Stores and PXT organizations), but each leader will communicate to their respective teams when we have the details nailed down.

  • 主句 We haven’t concluded yet 表示“截至当时还没有确定”；yet 与现在完成时的否定形式配合。
  • 括号内 we know that there will be reductions in our Stores and PXT organizations 补充已知信息；but 转回到未确定的具体人数。
  • when we have the details nailed down 是时间从句，说明各位领导者会在细节敲定后向各自团队沟通。
换到工作场景

We haven’t finalized exactly how many support shifts will be added next month.

句式仿写

比较两种写法，再试着换成自己的工作内容。

原来的写法

Amazon had already finalized the exact number of all future role reductions and planned to announce everything publicly first.

修改后的写法

The message said further reductions were expected in Stores and PXT, but the exact number of other affected roles had not been finalized. Leaders would communicate directly with affected employees before broader announcements.

邮件表示 Stores 和 PXT 还会有岗位缩减，但其他受影响岗位的具体数量尚未确定。领导者会先直接联系受影响员工，再作范围更广的公告。

试着替换信息
The message confirmed [known changes], while [the detail still pending] had not been finalized. Leaders would [next step] before [broader communication].

原文词汇 · 选学

incremental

原文词汇
ˌɪnkrəˈmentl　adjective
查看释义、搭配与用法

新增的；逐步增加的

incremental hires / changes

The message referred to pausing new incremental hires in the corporate workforce.

邮件提到暂停公司职能部门的新增招聘。

这里的 incremental 修饰 hires，表示新增招聘；不要把它理解为逐步减少员工。

workforce

原文词汇
ˈwɜːrkfɔːrs　noun
查看释义、搭配与用法

员工队伍；员工总数

workforce levels / workforce reduction

Leaders were reviewing workforce levels across their businesses.

各业务领导者正在评估员工规模。

在组织语境中指员工队伍或员工规模；具体人数需有原文依据。

prioritize

原文词汇
praɪˈɔːrətaɪz　verb
查看释义、搭配与用法

优先考虑；确定优先顺序

prioritize + customers / work / requests

The message said leaders would prioritize direct communication with affected employees.

邮件表示，领导者会优先直接联系受影响员工。

常用结构是 prioritize + 名词；此处说明沟通顺序。

challenging

原文词汇
ˈtʃælɪndʒɪŋ　adjective
查看释义、搭配与用法

艰难的；具有挑战的

a challenging economy / situation

The CEO described the economic situation as challenging.

CEO 将当时的经济形势描述为严峻。

原文说经济形势严峻，不等于每个业务都面临同样的具体问题。

reduction

原文词汇
rɪˈdʌkʃən　noun
查看释义、搭配与用法

缩减；减少

role reductions / cost reductions

The message said there would be more role reductions as planning continued.

邮件表示，随着规划继续，还会有更多岗位缩减。

role reduction 指岗位缩减；不能据此推断未公布的具体人数。

nail down

原文词汇
ˌneɪl ˈdaʊn　phrasal verb
查看释义、搭配与用法

敲定；确定

nail down the details / a date

Each leader would share details once they had nailed them down.

各位领导者会在细节敲定后进行沟通。

原文用 details nailed down，表示细节已确定；常见主动表达为 nail down the details。
