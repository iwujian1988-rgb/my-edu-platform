Prepare for Meetings with a Pre-read - Dropbox

会前预读材料怎样写，才能让大家带着想法来

DAY 076

. Pre-reads should achieve one goal: provide enough information to someone who may have little to no context so that they can meaningfully contribute in the meeting. Your pre-read should take the place of the common “Let me bring folks up to speed…” part of the meeting, saving five to ten minutes upfront. Attendees should be able to grasp the basics in five minutes or less. Try to format your pre-read in bullets to force clarity. Bonus points if you’re able to link out to meaningful components where folks can optionally learn more in advance (example: a Dropbox folder, a collaborative document like Paper document, or a ticket in a project management system).

An effective way to train those who routinely attend meetings you organize is to . By putting your pre-read materials in the shared agenda document that’s already attached to your meeting invite, you’re conditioning attendees to refer to the agenda before, during, and after meetings. It’s a good idea to share the pre-read one to two business days in advance. If all else fails, allocate time for silent reads to begin your meeting. When sharing, remind folks what they’re expected to read, why they’re expected to read it, and that you’re linking the same agenda doc that’s already in the meeting invite.

As you share the pre-read, ask folks to provide feedback if something is ambiguous or confusing. This not only helps you craft future pre-reads to be more concise and focused on the right things, but it also serves as a to actually read the pre-read. This tiny step adds a layer of accountability and touches on the . Attendees will recognize that you’re going above and beyond by providing a pre-read and asking for feedback, which in turn makes them more likely to reciprocate by reading (and hopefully, sharing a bit of input).

重点段落中文对照

01 在写作时追求理解与简洁的经验教训同样适用于此。预读材料应实现一个目标：为那些可能缺乏背景信息的人提供足够的信息，以便他们能在会议中有意义地做出贡献。你的预读材料应取代会议中常见的'让我向大家同步一下...'环节，从而节省五到十分钟的会议时间。与会者应在五分钟或更短时间内掌握基本内容。尽量使用项目符号格式来组织你的预读材料，以增强清晰度。如果你能够链接到有意义的组件（例如：一个Dropbox文件夹、像Paper这样的协作文档，或项目管理系统中的一个工单），那就更好了。

02 培训那些经常参加你组织的会议的一种有效方法是维护单一事实来源。通过将预读材料放入已附在会议邀请中的共享议程文档中，你正在引导与会者在会前、会中和会后都参考这份议程。提前一到两个工作日分享预读材料是个好主意。如果其他方法都失败了，则为会议开始留出默读时间。分享时，提醒大家需要阅读的内容、阅读的原因，以及你链接的是会议邀请中已有的同一份议程文档。

03 当你分享预读材料时，请要求大家对任何模糊或令人困惑的内容提供反馈。这不仅有助于你撰写更简洁、更聚焦于重点内容的未来预读材料，同时也作为一种温和的提醒，促使与会者实际阅读预读材料。这一小步骤增加了一层责任感，并触及了互惠的力量。与会者会认识到，你通过提供预读材料并征求反馈，已经付出了额外的努力，这反过来使他们更可能通过阅读（并希望提供一些意见）来回报你。

05

语法拆句

原文句子

Pre-reads should achieve one goal: provide enough information to someone who may have little to no context so that they can meaningfully contribute in the meeting.

  • 主句是“Pre-reads should achieve one goal”，one goal 是 achieve 的宾语。
  • 冒号后用 provide enough information 解释这个目标的具体内容；to someone 表示信息提供给谁。
  • “who may have little to no context”修饰 someone；“so that they can meaningfully contribute…”说明提供信息的目的。
换到工作场景

A five-minute pre-read can include the decision to make, a short summary, and the questions participants should review.
