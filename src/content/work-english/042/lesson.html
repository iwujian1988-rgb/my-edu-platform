微软前任 CEO 如何向员工介绍接班人（2014）

DAY 042

Date: Feb. 4, 2014 Subject: Satya Nadella – Microsoft’s New CEO

Today is an incredibly exciting day as we announce Satya Nadella as the new CEO of Microsoft. Satya will be a great CEO, and . You can read the full announcement here.

Satya is a proven leader. He’s got insights. He has a remarkable ability to see what’s going on in the market, to sense opportunity, and to really understand how we come together at Microsoft to execute against those opportunities in a collaborative way. I have worked closely with Satya for many years and I have seen these skills many times. He is not alone, though. Our Senior Leadership Team has never been stronger, and together this group will drive us forward.

Microsoft is one of the great companies in the world. I love this company. I love the bigness and boldness of what we do. I love the way we partner with other companies to come together to change the world. , from students in the classroom to consumers to small businesses to governments to the largest enterprises.

Above all, I love the spirit of this place, the passion, and the perseverance, which has been the cornerstone of our culture from the very beginning.

Stay focused and keep moving forward. I am excited about what we will do. Satya’s appointment confirms that.

Thanks for making Microsoft the most amazing place to work on the planet, and thanks for the chance to lead.

04

语法拆句

原文句子

Today is an incredibly exciting day as we announce Satya Nadella as the new CEO of Microsoft.

  • 主句“Today is an incredibly exciting day”是主系表结构：Today 作主语，is 是系动词，an incredibly exciting day 作表语。
  • “as we announce…”是 as 引导的时间从句，表示“在我们宣布……之际”；从句中 we announce Satya Nadella，as the new CEO of Microsoft 补充说明 Nadella 被任命的身份。
  • 注意两个 as 用法不同：前一个连接时间从句，后一个说明职位身份；此处前一个 as 不是因果关系。
换到工作场景

Today marks an important step as we welcome our new project lead.

句式仿写

比较两种写法，再试着换成自己的工作内容。

原来的写法

We should announce the new CEO without explaining his experience or how the handoff will work.

修改后的写法

Ballmer described Nadella as a proven leader with strong technical skills and business insight. He said Nadella understood the market and looked forward to seeing him bring Microsoft’s teams together around new opportunities.

Ballmer 将 Nadella 描述为一位经得起验证的领导者，具备很强的技术能力和商业洞察力。他表示，Nadella 了解市场，并期待看到他如何围绕新机遇凝聚微软团队。

试着替换信息

　adjective

强调某事物已被实践检验并证明有效，常用于描述领导者、方法或产品的可靠性
