Code of Conduct — expectations and interpersonal conflicts

DAY 099

We expect all active 37signals employees and contractors to:

. Approach work relationships defaulting to trust and positivity.

Work “in the open” and be open to teaching and learning from others.

, especially when it comes to differing viewpoints and experiences.

Gracefully accept constructive criticism and , and offer feedback in the same spirit.

An important note: . If you find a colleague rude or difficult to work with, you should address that with your manager or better yet with that colleague directly. Code of Conduct reports are reserved for serious transgressions — illegal or egregiously unethical behavior.

Source: Code of Conduct. 查看原文
重点段落中文对照

01 我们期望所有在职的 37signals 员工和承包商做到：

02 秉持善意。处理工作关系时，默认彼此信任、保持积极。

03 在公开中工作，并愿意向他人学习，也愿意教导他人。

04 保持尊重和同理心，尤其是面对不同的观点和经历时。

05 得体地接受建设性批评和直接反馈，并以同样的态度提供反馈。

06 需要特别说明：大多数人际冲突都达不到行为准则报告的程度。如果你觉得同事粗鲁或难相处，应与经理沟通，或者更好的是直接和那位同事沟通。行为准则报告适用于严重违规——违法或极不道德的行为。

04

语法拆句

原文句子

Gracefully accept constructive criticism and direct feedback, and offer feedback in the same spirit.

  • Gracefully accept 和 offer 是并列的祈使结构，分别提出接受和给出反馈的行为要求。
  • constructive criticism and direct feedback 是 accept 的并列宾语。
  • in the same spirit 指以同样尊重、建设性的态度提供反馈。
换到工作场景

I’m assuming good intentions, and I’d like to share direct feedback about the review comments. Could you point to one example that needs more detail?

I’ll assume good intentions and address the issue directly and respectfully with my colleague.

中译英 · 先说，再看答案

1. 我先按善意理解，不过反复来电打断了我的分析。

2. 我们能否约定每天下午三点书面更新一次进度？

3. 如果遇到阻碍，我会提前说明。

参考表达

1. I’m assuming good intentions, but the repeated calls interrupt my analysis.

说明：assume good intentions 表示先按善意理解；后半句指出具体行为和工作影响。

2. Could we agree on one written status update at 3 p.m. each day?

说明：Could we… 礼貌提出方案；one written status update 提供明确而可预测的同步方式。

3. I’ll flag any blocker sooner.

说明：flag a blocker 表示说明会影响进度的问题；sooner 表明不等到固定更新时间才告知。
