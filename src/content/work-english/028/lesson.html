情景练习（模拟） · First Week Project Kickoff Challenges

以下人物与情节为模拟内容，用于练习表达。

During the first week of the new project, Sarah and Tom were responsible for organizing the playbook content and building the initial product backlog while also discussing team agreements.

Sarah prioritized creating a comprehensive playbook for quick reference, while Tom insisted on focusing on building the product backlog first due to the upcoming client deadline.

Sarah suggested, 'Let's split our time - I'll finalize the playbook structure while you start on the backlog, then we can review together tomorrow to ensure alignment.'

原文词汇 · 选学

playbook

原文词汇
/ˈpleɪbʊk/　noun
查看释义、搭配与用法

手册；指南

digital playbook, sales playbook, operations playbook

The team created a comprehensive digital playbook to document all standard operating procedures.

团队创建了一个全面的数字化手册来记录所有标准操作流程。

在商业和技术环境中，playbook通常指包含策略、流程和最佳实践的参考文档。

可发现性；可检索性

The company invested in a new search system to improve the discoverability of technical documentation.

公司投资于新的搜索系统，以提高技术文档的可发现性。

在信息管理中，discoverability指用户能够轻松找到所需信息的能力。

05

语法拆句

原文句子

Build a Product Backlog Set up a project in your chosen project management tool (ex.

  • "Build a Product Backlog" 是一个祈使句，作为指令，其中"Build"是动词，"a Product Backlog"是名词短语作宾语
  • "Set up a project" 是另一个祈使句，与前面的祈使句并列，其中"Set up"是动词短语，"a project"是名词短语作宾语
  • "in your chosen project management tool" 是介词短语，修饰"Set up"，表示设置项目的工具，其中"your chosen"是过去分词短语作定语修饰"project management tool"

06

句式仿写

比较两种写法，再试着换成自己的工作内容。

原来的写法

You do both the backlog and the playbook. I’ll check them tomorrow.

修改后的写法

Let’s split the work: I’ll organize the playbook structure while you start the backlog. We can review both tomorrow.

我们分一下工：我整理工程手册的结构，你先建待办列表。明天我们一起检查。

试着替换信息
Let’s split the work: I’ll handle [task A], while you take [task B]. Then we can [review together].
